# Ms. Bee & Co.

A website demo for Ms. Bee & Co., a Lebanese home-care and cleaning-products brand. It presents the product range in a polished, easy-to-explore online home as the business grows beyond social media.

## What the site showcases

- Household cleaning essentials for the bathroom, kitchen, and home.
- A browsable product catalog with category filters and product-specific imagery.
- Practical cleaning guidance, with English and Arabic support.
- Direct links to Ms. Bee & Co. on Instagram and Facebook.
- The brand message: 🇱🇧 Proudly made by Chemical Engineers.

This is a business website demo. Product availability and enquiries are directed to the brand's social channels.

## Technology

- HTML5
- Tailwind CSS (CDN)
- Vanilla JavaScript
